Cash Care 📊💰
A Personal Finance Management App

Overview
Cash Care is a personal finance management app designed to help users track expenses, manage budgets, and gain financial insights through intuitive graphs and analytics. Whether you're budgeting for daily expenses or planning long-term financial goals, Cash Care provides a simple yet powerful solution to keep your finances on track.

Key Features
✅ Income & Expense Tracking – Easily record your earnings and expenses with categorized inputs.
📊 Graphical Insights – View your financial data through interactive charts and graphs.
💡 Budget Planning – Set spending limits and receive alerts to stay within budget.
📅 Transaction History – Keep a detailed log of your financial activities.
🔒 Secure & Private – Your financial data stays protected with robust security measures.

Technology Stack
Frontend: Flutter (Dart),
State Management: BLoC,
Database: Local storage / SQLite (if applicable),
Version Control: Git, GitHub,
Future Enhancements
🚀 Cloud Sync & Backup – Securely store data across devices.
📈 AI-Based Financial Insights – Get personalized recommendations based on spending patterns.
💳 Expense Categorization with AI – Auto-classify transactions for better tracking.
